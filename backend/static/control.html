How a report reaches the map

  1. A citizen reports a problemFrom the public map or Telegram
  2. Control room checks itPlausible? Then send it to field police, or publish now if urgentThis desk
  3. Field police confirm it at the spotOnly now does it reach the public map
  4. Commuters are reroutedLive, by vehicle type
